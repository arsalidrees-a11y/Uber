Night visibility basics

▶

Most night-time incidents happen in the first ten minutes after sunset, while your eyes are still adjusting.

Why sunset is the risk window
Your pupils need roughly twenty minutes to fully dilate. Oncoming headlights reset that adjustment each time.
Cleaning your windscreen
Interior film scatters light far more than exterior dirt. Wipe the inside of the glass weekly.

How long do your eyes need to adjust?

Your pupils need about twenty minutes. Leave extra following distance in that window.
